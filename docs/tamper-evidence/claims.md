# Claims table for sales (TAQ-23)

Each claim in [explainer.md](explainer.md) / [explainer.id.md](explainer.id.md) has an id here, with the evidence in the repo
and the wording to use or avoid. Evidence = decision numbers in [DECISIONS.md](../../DECISIONS.md) and test names (Go, under
`backend/`). A claim without evidence does not belong in the explainer; if a test is renamed, update this file.
Short names: `fieldauth/`, `service/`, `proofpack/` = `backend/internal/<name>`; `cmd/garda-verify` = `backend/cmd/garda-verify`; `integration/` = `backend/internal/integration` (needs `-tags integration`), `chainproof/` = `backend/internal/chainproof`.

Overall line: **"tamper-evident, anchored outside the app"**. Never "tamper-proof", "cannot be changed", "no need to trust the vendor".

## What Garda does (can be said)

| Id | Claim | Evidence | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Attendance, patrol scans, incidents and incident corrections are each added to a per-site chain, in the same database transaction as the write; a write that is rolled back leaves no entry. | D-069. `integration/`: `TestChainLinksPerSite`, `TestChainCoversOtherWritePaths`, `TestChainRolledBackWriteLeavesNoEntry`, `TestChainConcurrentWritesStayValid` | "Every record is chained when it is saved." | "Everything in Garda is chained" (guard profiles, photos and settings are not). |
| G2 | An edited or deleted record, an edited, deleted or reordered chain entry, or a forged insert is detected, also when done directly in the database. | D-069, D-073. `integration/`: `TestChainDetectsEditedRecord`, `TestChainDetectsDeletedRecord`, `TestChainDetectsEditedChainEntry`, `TestChainDetectsDeletedChainEntry`, `TestChainDetectsReorderedEntries`, `TestChainDetectsForgedInsert`, `TestChainBundleDetectsTamperingInTheDatabase`; `chainproof/`: `TestEditedRecordIsFirstBreak`, `TestDeletedRecordIsFirstBreak` | "An edit is detected and the first changed record is named." | "Nobody can edit." The edit itself is not prevented. |
| G3 | A correction made through the app adds a new entry; the old entries stay, so the history is visible. | D-069, D-074 (chain section). `integration/`: `TestChainLegitimateChangesStayValid`; `chainproof/`: `TestSupersededEntries` | "Corrections are shown as history, not hidden." | "Old versions of a record are kept": only the chain entry stays; the old record text is not kept (D-073). |
| G4 | A separate signer process signs each site's newest entry about hourly, at the site's day end and on demand, with a key and a database login the app does not have. | D-071. `integration/`: `TestSignerSignsAndStoresHead`, `TestRolesAfterHardening` | "Signed by a separate service the app cannot reach." | "Hardware-protected key": it is a file key on its own volume (D-071). |
| G5 | A history that is rewritten and re-chained (so the chain alone looks fine) is caught by the earlier signed heads. | D-071. `integration/`: `TestSignedHeadsDetectRewrittenPast`, `TestChainBundleDetectsTamperingInTheDatabase` (case "rewritten and re-chained past"); `chainproof/`: `TestForgedPastIsCaughtBySignedHead`, `TestHeadForAnotherHashIsCaught` | "Rewriting history after a signature shows." | "Cannot be rewritten" (see L1). |
| G6 | The signer refuses a lower sequence number or a different hash at a signed one, and keeps its own record on its own volume, so wiping the database copy of the heads does not make it forget. | D-071. `integration/`: `TestSignerRefusesLowerSeq`, `TestSignerRefusesConflictingHashAtSignedSeq`, `TestSignerRemembersWhenTheOwnerWipesSignedHeads`, `TestSignerRefusesLowerSeqFromItsOwnRecord` | "The signer keeps its own memory." | "The signer cannot be fooled" (see L1). |
| G7 | A client can check an export offline with `garda-verify`, no account, no internet, using the fingerprint they hold; a wrong fingerprint fails (exit 4); a forged signature or keyring fails. | D-073. `cmd/garda-verify`: `TestExitCodes`; `chainproof/`: `TestWrongFingerprintIsCaught`, `TestForgedHeadSignature`, `TestForgedKeyringIsCaught`; `integration/`: `TestChainBundleVerifiesOffline` | "You can check it yourself, without our systems." | "Without trusting anyone": you trust the signer key you pinned. |
| G8 | The verifier never gives a plain `VALID` for an empty export, entries after the last signature, records changed again outside the file, or a date range it was not told to check; the tool says so and exits non-zero. | D-073 (review hardening). `chainproof/`: `TestEmptyBundleProvesNothing`, `TestAttackerEmptyBundle`, `TestAttackerTailTruncation`, `TestAttackerCutAtAnOlderHead`, `TestAttackerSupersededEscape`, `TestAttackerLateAnchor`, `TestAttackerManifestRangeLies`, `TestFlagsAloneNeverGivePlainValid` | "It says what it could not prove." | "A valid result means nothing is missing" (it does not: D-073 "Does not do"). |
| P1 | The monthly proof-pack PDF states `UNSIGNED TAIL`, `CHAIN CHECK FAILED` or `NO ACTIVITY` instead of "passed" when that is the case, and tells the reader to run `garda-verify` with their own fingerprint. | D-074. `proofpack/`: `TestUnsignedTailIsNeverPresentedAsVerified`, `TestFailedAndNoActivityPacks`, `TestLastPageHasInstructionsAndLimits`; `integration/`: `TestProofPackUnsignedTailAndEmptyMonth`, `TestProofPackPrintedCommandRuns` | "The proof pack is built from the same check; the client re-runs it themselves." | "The PDF proves the month" (its figures are not signed: L4). |
| F1 | Checkpoint QR codes have a time window; stale, invalid, late or clock-ahead scans are accepted and flagged, not dropped; marking a flag as reviewed writes a separate row and not the flagged record. | D-072, D-070. `fieldauth/`: `TestQRWindowedPayload`, `TestQRWindowContains`, `TestQRWindowAccepts`; `service/`: `TestResolveCheckpointQRWindow`; `integration/`: `TestSignedQRCodes`, `TestPlausibilityFlags`, `TestFlagDashboard`. The review-mark claim rests on D-070 and the `flag_reviews` design; no test reads the chain after a review. | "Odd scans are flagged for review, not lost." | "Prevents fake patrols": flags are signals, see L5-L7. |

## What Garda does not guarantee (must be said when asked, and shown in the explainer box)

| Id | Limit | Evidence | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Host root plus the signer's key volume can sign a rewritten history. Pinned fingerprint, the client's own earlier exports and key handovers in the client's presence limit it. A key change after loss needs the client to pin again. | D-071 ("What remains", "Does not buy"), D-073 ("Does not do"); `docs/chain-verification.md`. `integration/`: `TestSignerCompromisedKeyStartsNewEpoch` (re-pin after a new epoch) | "Raises the bar from database owner to server root plus the signer's key." | "Even we cannot change it." |
| L2 | Entries after the newest signed head are not vouched for (signing is about hourly); the verifier reports `UNSIGNED TAIL` (exit 7), or `STALE` (exit 6) when that head is old; a cut-short export looks the same. | D-073. `chainproof/`: `TestAttackerTailTruncation`, `TestStale`; `integration/`: `TestChainBundleUnsignedAndStale` | "Records of the last hour may not be signed yet." | "Real-time proof." |
| L3 | A genuine old export stays valid for what it holds; without an expected range it is `RANGE NOT AUTHENTICATED` (exit 11), and the tool prints the export and signature times. | D-073. `chainproof/`: `TestAttackerReplay` | "Check the export date." | "Valid means current." |
| L4 | The figures in the proof-pack PDF are read from the database when the PDF is made and are not signed; the PDF says so. | D-074 ("Figures are not signed"). `proofpack/`: `TestFiguresAreNotSignedAndSaySo` | "Totals are a convenience; the signed part is the chain section." | "A signed PDF" / "signed report". |
| L5 | A photo of a printed QR can be replayed until the window ends (default 30 days) plus 10 minutes of clock skew and 24 hours of offline grace. | D-072 ("Replay horizon"). `fieldauth/`: `TestQRWindowAccepts`; `service/`: `TestResolveCheckpointQRWindow` | "A printed code is valid for a set time window." | "A QR code cannot be copied." |
| L6 | A guard who is offline or sets the tablet clock back and lies about the time can replay a photo of a code until that horizon; `late_sync`, `clock_ahead`, GPS and photo checks are signals, not a block. | D-072 ("Remaining limit", "Known limits"). `service/`: `TestTimeAndClockInFlags`, `TestScanFlags` | "Suspicious timing is flagged." | "A guard cannot fake a patrol." |
| L7 | The chain proves a record is unchanged since it was saved, not that it was true when entered; the app role can still write a false state as a new, visible entry. | D-069 (scope), D-071 ("Does not buy"). `integration/`: `TestChainLegitimateChangesStayValid` (a change through the app is a new entry) | "Shows what was recorded and when it changed." | "Proves what happened on site." |
| L8 | Only a fingerprint the client got out of band counts; the one in the PDF or in a bundle is the server's own claim. `--trust-first-use` is weaker and says so; without `--pin` the tool refuses. | D-073 ("Trust"), D-074 ("Limits kept"). `cmd/garda-verify`: `TestExitCodes` | "Keep the fingerprint from installation somewhere we cannot change." | "Use the fingerprint in the report." |
| L9 | Existing rows were chained by the migration (`origin = 'backfill'`): they are vouched for only as they were on that day. | D-069. `integration/`: `TestChainMigrationBackfillsExistingRows` | "From the day the chain was switched on." | "The whole history is proven." |
| L10 | Export downloads are not audit-logged yet; the bundle holds personal data (admin and supervisor only). | D-073 ("Known gaps") | State it if asked about data access. | "Every access is logged." |
